Market Hall

An artisan market barn planned for Cumming Avenue, with storefronts for local makers, boutique businesses, and food vendors.

  • 7,134 SF
  • 8 tenant spaces
  • 611 to 821 SF each
Share your business idea See the floor plan

Future concept Plans are still taking shape.

A market barn on Cumming Avenue

Market Hall sits on Great Western Crossing’s neighborhood commercial site, between Middlebrook’s first neighborhood, downtown Cumming, and Middlebrook Farm. The market barn fronts Cumming Avenue with views of the Farm to the south, bringing a rural sensibility and scale to future retail.

Inside, the barn envisions shops for local artisans, boutique businesses, and food vendors, all in an intimate setting to introduce their craft to the community. Small tenant spaces give start-ups and small businesses a chance to sell their goods in a storefront setting.

Between the neighborhood and the Farm

The single-family homes and townhomes next door are within walking and biking distance. Bike paths will connect the market barn to the Great Western Trail, downtown Cumming, and Middlebrook’s neighborhoods to the south and east.

Nearby: Great Western Crossing, the Great Western Trail, the Middlebrook Mercantile, and Middlebrook Farm.
