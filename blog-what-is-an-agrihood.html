Blog · Living at Middlebrook · October 2026

What is an Agrihood?

A neighborhood built around a working farm, where the garden, the trail, and Main Street are all part of an ordinary day.

Most neighborhoods are built around a golf course, a clubhouse, or a pool. An Agrihood is built around a farm. At Middlebrook, in Cumming, Iowa, that means a two-acre Town Garden at the center of everything, with homes, trails, and a small-town Main Street growing up around it.

The idea is simple: when fresh food, open space, and your neighbors are a short walk away, everyday life gets a little slower and a little better.

A day in the Agrihood

Start with a walk. Middlebrook Meadow is more than 50 acres of native grasses and wildflowers, with mowed trails, a pond, and three trailheads. The Great Western Trail runs right through the Town Center, paved and ready for a ride into Cumming or on to Des Moines.

Stop by the Farm Stand on the way home. From May through October it is stocked with produce and eggs grown right here at the Town Garden, following organic practices. If you would rather grow your own, rent a garden plot, or one in the high tunnel that keeps growing through the winter. In summer, cut a bouquet from the you-pick flower field.

On Friday nights from mid-May through September, the Farm Grounds fill up for Fridays at the Farm, with live music, food trucks, and farmers’ market vendors. Any other evening, the Merc pours cocktails and wine in Cumming’s 1900 one-room schoolhouse.

Room to grow, on purpose

At full build-out, Middlebrook will cover about 800 acres, with 30 percent set aside for agriculture and recreation. That land is part of the plan from the start, not whatever is left over.

The homes are built with the same care. The Townhomes, Cottages, and Lofts heat and cool with geothermal energy, and it is an option in every new build. The Town Center’s commercial buildings run on solar from their own roofs. And design guidelines keep porches facing the street and garages out of the way, so no two homes on a block look alike.

A place to belong

An Agrihood is really about people. Neighbors meet over garden plots and at the Farm Stand, wave from their porches, and see each other on Friday nights. As Middlebrook’s founder put it: “I envision a different future where development and agriculture unite to build a better community.”
