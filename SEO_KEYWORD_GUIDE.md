# Middlebrook SEO Keyword Guide

## Core brand and location strategy

Use Middlebrook as the main brand, with Cumming, Iowa as the primary location anchor. Use Des Moines, West Des Moines, and Norwalk as secondary local references when they fit naturally.

### Recommended primary keyword pattern
- Middlebrook Agrihood
- Middlebrook homes
- Middlebrook community
- Middlebrook in Cumming, Iowa
- Middlebrook agrihood in Cumming, Iowa

### Secondary local keyword references
- near Des Moines, Iowa
- West Des Moines area
- Norwalk area
- greater Des Moines region

### Best use pattern
Use the brand name and targeted local terms together, not repeatedly in a row.

Examples:
- Middlebrook Agrihood in Cumming, Iowa
- Middlebrook homes near Des Moines
- Middlebrook community in the West Des Moines area
- Middlebrook farm lifestyle near Norwalk

## What to avoid
- Repeating Middlebrook excessively in a single block of text
- Stuffing multiple city names into a single sentence
- Using location names without context or readability
- Writing awkward phrases like “Middlebrook Des Moines West Des Moines Norwalk”

## Recommended balance
For homepage and important landing pages:
- Use Middlebrook naturally in the title and first paragraph
- Include Cumming, Iowa as the anchor location
- Use Des Moines, West Des Moines, or Norwalk only in supporting context

## Example page language
- Middlebrook Agrihood is a multigenerational community in Cumming, Iowa, offering a walkable farm lifestyle and thoughtfully designed homes.
- Middlebrook homes near Des Moines provide a quieter, more connected way of living rooted in community and the land.
- Located in the West Des Moines area, Middlebrook brings together neighborhood living, farm culture, and easy access to the region.

## Summary
The strongest strategy is:
- Middlebrook is the lead brand
- Cumming, Iowa is the main location
- Des Moines, West Des Moines, and Norwalk are secondary local references used naturally, not excessively
