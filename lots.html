Lots in Great Western Crossing

Build your home in Middlebrook’s first residential neighborhood, with tree-lined streets, walkable amenities, and quick access to I-35 and West Des Moines. Bring your own builder.

Select a lot on the map.

Flat, walkout, or daylight

Flat
A level lot. The lower level sits fully below ground, with standard basement windows.
Walkout
The lot slopes away toward the back, so the lower level opens at ground level onto the backyard.
Daylight
A gentler slope, with larger windows that bring daylight into the lower level.

Planning a build

Every new home in Great Western Crossing follows the neighborhood’s design guidelines: front porches, pitched roofs, and garages kept from dominating the street. Builders can also choose a geothermal system.
