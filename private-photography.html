Your photos, on the Farm

Middlebrook Agrihood is the perfect setting for your photography session. Book your engagement, senior, or family photos among the gardens, the Meadow, and the Farmhouse porch.

  • $50 per hour
  • Outdoor sessions
  • By appointment
Request a session Session policies

Where you can shoot

Every session includes outdoor access to Middlebrook Farm (the Farmhouse, the barn, the gardens, and the grounds), Middlebrook Meadow, and Middlebrook Mercantile.

  • The Farmhouse

    A deep front porch, white clapboard, and summer hydrangeas.

  • The gardens

    Flower rows, the high tunnel, and the Town Garden beds.

  • The Meadow

    Native prairie grasses, mowed trails, and the pond.

  • The Mercantile

    The restored one-room schoolhouse and its bell tower.

Recent sessions at Middlebrook

Engagement photos by Claire Zellmer. Family photos by April + August Photography.

How booking works

Sessions are for personal, non-commercial photos, and every session is scheduled by appointment with approval from Middlebrook Farm.

Request a dateFill out the request form with your photographer, the type of session, how many people are coming, and your arrival and departure times.

We confirmOur team reaches out to confirm availability and finalize your booking.

Pay aheadSession fees are $50 per hour, paid in advance by check or credit card. Fees are non-refundable.

Check in at the MercantileEveryone in your party checks in at Middlebrook Mercantile on arrival and on the way out, and your photography badge stays visible the whole time. Need to change? Use the Mercantile restrooms.

Session policies

Hours

Sessions begin and end during Middlebrook Mercantile’s business hours, based on availability. Requests outside those hours are considered one at a time.

Where you can go

Photography on private property around Middlebrook Farm, including Wilson’s Orchard & Farm, is not allowed. Please don’t enter or stand in any garden beds or planted areas. Construction or maintenance may be underway, and any roped-off or blocked areas are closed; we will let you know about planned work before your booking. The Mercantile restrooms are the only place on site to change clothes.

Props, food, and drones

Props and décor need approval. Nothing may be attached to any surface, and no signs or banners may be placed on the grounds without approval. Decorations must be fireproof, so no candles, lanterns, or sparklers. No food or alcoholic drinks on the grounds during sessions. Drones need pre-approval and proper insurance.

Fees and responsibility

All fees and policies are subject to change and may vary with the complexity of the request. The person booking agrees to hold Middlebrook Farm, its officers, staff, and agents harmless from all claims while on the grounds, and is responsible for any disturbance or damage caused by their party. Middlebrook Farm may ask for a security deposit, and any damage would be deducted from it.
