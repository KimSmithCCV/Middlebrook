The Middlebrook Farmhouse

A turn-of-the-century farmhouse on Middlebrook Farm. Book the whole house for a weeknight stay, or gather here for a shower, a class, or a dinner party.

Check dates and book Plan an event

Yours for the night

Most weeknights, you can book the entire Farmhouse for an overnight stay. Settle into the rich history and rustic elegance of the house, from its original woodwork and built-in cabinets to the brick fireplace and a dining table long enough for everyone.

Spend the day on the trail, in the Meadow, or on the front porch, then walk across the street for a glass of wine.

Across the street, the Mercantile

The Farmhouse sits across the street from the Middlebrook Mercantile, Cumming’s historic one-room schoolhouse. Restored and reopened as a wine and cocktail bar, it greets you at night with exclusive wines and flavor-forward cocktails.

Also close by: the Great Western Trail, Mayor Tom Becker Park, Middlebrook Meadow, and the Town Garden.

Host it at the Farmhouse

With its timeless beauty and rustic charm, the Farmhouse is the backdrop for community events and private rentals at Middlebrook Farm. Imagine hosting:

  • Baby showers
  • Wedding showers
  • Private classes
  • Dinner parties
  • Club get-togethers
  • Friends weekends

And so much more, in this charming and historic setting.
