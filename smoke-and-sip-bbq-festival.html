Smoke & Sip BBQ Festival

Pitmasters, cold drinks, live music, and yard games on the Farm at Middlebrook.

Saturday, May 15, 2027 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM · Free admission

This ain’t your county fair cookoff. It’s a bare-knuckle BBQ festival on Middlebrook’s working farm, fueled by smoked meats and cold drinks.

2027 is the third annual Smoke & Sip, and each year has been bigger and better than the year before.

The pitmasters

Six local pitmasters each bring their best dish and side. Walk the rows, eat what looks good, and cast your vote for People’s Choice before you go.

$5
a dish
$3
a side

It’s an affordable way to roll deep, eat big, and own the afternoon, your way.

The 2027 pitmasters and musicians are still being booked. Get the lineup by email

Wet your whistle

Local breweries, distilleries, and cideries set up tents to pour samples of their finest. The beer tent sells beer, wine, cider, and seltzer, and most of the pitmasters sell soft drinks, too.

Local vendors set up samples of their own, everything from hot sauce to Bloody Mary mix.

Pouring in past years

  • Cumming Beer Co.
  • Iowa Distilling
  • Wilson’s Orchard
  • Revelton
  • Lua Brewing
  • and more

Chicken Sh!t Bingo

Free entry

Pick a square on the board, then let the chicken decide. Wherever it does its business, that square wins.

Live music all day

Live local music plays on The Farmhouse porch stage all afternoon, so grab a plate and pull up a chair.

Games in the yard

Challenge your buddies to yard games on the lawn between plates.

Plan your visit

When
Saturday, May 15, 2027
11:00 AM to 3:00 PM
Where
The Farm Grounds
4300 Cumming Ave, Cumming
Cost
Free to get in. Pay as you go for food and drinks, with cash or card.
Seating
Seating is limited, so bring your own lawn chairs or blankets. To reserve a picnic table or the Farmhouse, email Events@MiddlebrookFarm.com.
Parking
In Town Center (south of G14/Cumming Avenue) along S. 44th Street or Main Street, or in the field parking reached from Town Center. Signs will guide you, and spots near the Farm Grounds go fast, so plan to arrive close to 11:00 AM.
Accessibility
Smoke & Sip is an outdoor event held on natural terrain, including grass, mulch, and gravel, with some uneven areas. There will be porta-potties available on site, including a handicap-accessible unit. Questions or need accommodations? Reach out to Events@MiddlebrookFarm.com before you visit.
