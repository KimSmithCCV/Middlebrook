Apartment living in the Agrihood

The Lofts bring a modern loft feel to a quiet, nature-connected setting. Choose a garden-level walkout, a first-floor home with 12-foot ceilings, or a second-floor home overlooking the Great Western Trail.

Step outside and you are a two-minute walk from Middlebrook Meadow and the Farm, a minute from Fridays at the Farm, and close to the shops on Main Street. Water, sewer, and trash are included in rent.
