Luxury geothermal townhomes

By tapping into natural energy, these geothermal townhomes use the stable and consistent temperature of the earth. Homeowners enjoy superior heating and cooling year-round and a comfortable, tranquil home. Ground Breaker Homes, Diligent Development, and Orca Energy are proud to bring the first of its kind community to central Iowa.

Built by Ground Breaker Homes
Front porches, pitched roofs, and simple farmhouse forms, all built to Middlebrook’s design guidelines.
One- and two-story plans
Ranch plans keep the main living on one level, and some include a finished lower level. Two-story plans put three bedrooms upstairs.

Around the Townhomes

Courtyards and gathering spaces
Shared neighborhood courtyards, gathering spaces, and fire pits give neighbors a place to meet up right outside their doors.
A native prairie next door
Right next to the Townhomes, a native prairie does double duty. It holds stormwater as the neighborhood’s water retention area, and it gives the Townhomes a stretch of native grasses for neighbors.

The outside work, handled
