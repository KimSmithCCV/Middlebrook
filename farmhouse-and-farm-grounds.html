Host it on the Farm.

Middlebrook is the backdrop for all kinds of gatherings, from farm-to-table dinners and graduation open houses to showers, reunions, and weddings. Gather inside the turn-of-the-century Farmhouse, out on the side yard under the bistro lights, or in the garden.

We help with the details

Our event team is here to make hosting effortless, with help on event layout, catering recommendations, and all the details that bring your event to life.

Bring any catererMiddlebrook Farm has an open catering policy. Use a preferred caterer, bring your own food, or cook in the Farmhouse kitchen.

Bar service from the MercantileMiddlebrook Mercantile can pour beer, seltzer, and wine at your event (menu on request). Service is $100 for one bartender, with a $500 alcohol purchase minimum. When the Mercantile is serving, no outside alcohol is allowed on-site, though guests may bring their own non-alcoholic drinks. For larger groups, Middlebrook Farm may require Mercantile bar service.

Or bring your ownBeer and wine are welcome at any rental. Liquor is not allowed on-site.

Good to know

Cancellations and weather

Rentals are 100% refundable until 30 days before your event. Within 30 days, 50% of the rental fee is refundable. There is no indoor backup in case of severe weather, and rentals cancelled for weather receive a 50% refund.

Hours and insurance

All private rentals end by 10:00 PM. Proof of insurance is required for every private rental.

Sharing the Farm

Middlebrook’s garden and Farm Stand stay open to the public during private rentals.

Tents and decorations

Pop-up tents are welcome during your rental hours. They must be staked or weighted, and cannot go under the bistro lights. No open flames, glitter, confetti, silly string, or similar items on Middlebrook grounds, and no nails, staples, or tacks when decorating.

Cleanup

At the end of your event, bag your trash, replace the trash can liners (we provide them), and take everything to the dumpster on-site.
