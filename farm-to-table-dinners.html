Farm to Table Dinners

Seasonal dinners on Middlebrook Farm, in the Farmhouse or out in the garden, with live music and wine pairings from Capital Call Vintners.

Upcoming dinners Host your own

A seat at the table

Farm to Table Dinners bring Middlebrook’s chef in residence, Grant Gillon, winner of Season 13 of MasterChef, to a long table on the Farm with a small group of guests. Each course comes with a wine pairing from Capital Call Vintners, and live music plays through the evening.

Outdoor dinners often end as they should, around a bonfire with more wine and new friends.

Wine pairings from Capital Call Vintners

Every course is paired with a Pacific Northwest wine from Capital Call Vintners, grown on its estate vineyards in Oregon’s Columbia Gorge and Walla Walla Valley AVAs and sourced from the highly coveted Red Mountain AVA.

The wines have earned 91 points from Decanter, 92 points from Wine Enthusiast, a 97-point Double Platinum from Great Northwest Wine, and Double Golds at the San Francisco Chronicle Wine Competition, the largest competition of North American wines.

The winery’s owners live just down the road, so you can find the wines year-round at the Middlebrook Mercantile or from Capital Call Vintners.

Host your own dinner

Rent the Farmhouse, the side yard, or the garden and plan a farm to table dinner of your own, or bring in your own tables and chairs for something bigger. Every rental has a 3-hour minimum, and rental hours include your setup and teardown. Middlebrook residents receive 20% off all rental rates.
