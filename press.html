About Middlebrook

A walkable agrihood in Cumming, Iowa.

Middlebrook Agrihood brings homes, local businesses, open space, and agriculture together in a walkable community in Cumming, Iowa. The community makes significant use of geothermal energy, alongside shared green space, The Farm, and nearby trails.

Home types range from townhomes and cottages to villas and lofts, with walkable connections to Town Center and community events.
