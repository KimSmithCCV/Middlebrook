Fridays at the Farm

Live music, food trucks, and farmers’ market vendors on the Farm Grounds, every Friday from mid-May through September.

2026 Music and Food Truck Lineup

Kick off the weekend

Experience Middlebrook the way neighbors do, with a Friday evening on the Farm Grounds.

Experience Middlebrook by joining us on Fridays for live music, food trucks, farmers’ market vendors, fresh organic produce, and more at Fridays at the Farm! Middlebrook welcomes friends and families to kick off their weekends by enjoying this free event at Middlebrook Farm every Friday mid-May through the end of September from 4:30 PM to 7:30 PM.

Plan your Friday

Good to know

Food and drink

The Middlebrook Mercantile Wine Wagen pours wine by the bottle or glass, and the beer tent next to it serves a variety of beer and seltzers. Each week, a rotating selection of local food trucks will be present.

You’re welcome to bring your own picnic to The Farm, but no outside alcoholic drinks or coolers are permitted.

Vendors

Each week, browse a wide selection of local vendors in our Vendor Village, located just south of the Farmhouse stage. There you’ll find baked goods, original art, handmade jewelry, and more.

2026 Farmers’ Market Vendor Schedule

Accessibility

Fridays at the Farm is an outdoor event held on natural terrain, including grass, mulch, and gravel, with some uneven areas. There will be porta-potties available on site, including a handicap-accessible unit. Please note that accessing the Farm Stand requires stepping up into the structure.

Questions or need accommodations? Reach out to Events@MiddlebrookFarm.com before you visit.

Parking

Park in Middlebrook Town Center (south of G14/Cumming Avenue) along S. 44th Street or Main Street, or in our field parking (accessible from Town Center; signs will guide you).

Parking near the Farm Grounds fills up quickly, so we recommend arriving as close to the 4:30 PM start time as possible to secure a nearby spot. Live nearby? Walk or bike over on the sidewalks and the Great Western Trail.

Seating

Bring your own lawn chairs or blankets, or reserve a picnic table or the Farmhouse. Reservations are first come, first served.

Fridays at the Farm 101

Reserve a space

Save your seat at the Farm

Skip the lawn-chair shuffle. Reserve a picnic table with your name on it, or take over the Farmhouse for a VIP night with your group.

2027 coming soon 2026 reservations are closed. Check back for the 2027 season.

Picnic table

Look for the decorated picnic table with your name on it.

  • Seats up to 6 people
  • Reserve more than one for a bigger group
  • Most of the lawn is shaded by mid-evening

The Farmhouse

Throw open the windows and hear the music from the front porch while your group relaxes indoors on the Farmhouse’s first floor.

  • Room for 20 to 50+ guests, seated and standing
  • Lounge areas and air conditioning
  • A private, indoor restroom
  • Bring your own food
  • Pre-order drinks and find the fridge stocked when you arrive, no waiting in line

The Farmhouse nearly sold out for 2026, so plan early.

Ask about 2027 reservations

A member of the Farm team follows up to confirm each reservation and collect payment. Requests close 48 hours before each Friday. If weather cancels a Friday, we will help you move your reservation to another week.

Fun for the kids, too

Fridays at the Farm is kid friendly from start to finish. Kids can kick a ball around on the soccer field, jump into a round of gaga ball, and run free across the open lawns while the grown-ups enjoy the music.
