# Middlebrook Brand & Language Style Guide

## Naming shorthand
Shorthand property/place names can be used on Middlebrook's own channels (website, social, email) without first spelling out the full name. For outside publications, give the full name first.

- "Agrihood" — capitalized on its own or as part of "Middlebrook Agrihood." In a sentence: "life in the Agrihood."
- Community can be called "Middlebrook" after first being introduced as "Middlebrook Agrihood."

## Copy style rules
- Use Oxford commas.
- Hyphenate ranges like "one- to three-bedroom."
- No "&" in body paragraphs (fine in headers/sub-headers).
- Times: "X:XX" or "XX:XX" format, with AM/PM on both times in a range (can break this if space-constrained).
- Dates: "December 2, 2025" — not "December 2nd, 2025."
- "Cumming, IA" (don't abbreviate "Street").
- No em dashes in generated copy (client preference).
- Avoid future-tense copy describing the community ("the agrihood will be...") — write in present tense; this is an established, 7-year-old community.
- Never frame a specific home type as being "for" a specific age group or family role — describe configuration/function instead (fair housing).

## Brand visual system
- **Primary colors:** Black `#000000`, White `#FFFFFF`, Green `#898F70`, Mustard `#A9A47C` (approx.), Mustard Shade `#F3E9D8`
- **Secondary colors:** Burnt Umber `#AC8561`, Dusty Pink `#BAA894`, Chestnut `#894A3A`, Grey Blue `#6B7276`, Forest Green `#3E4332`, Muted Brown `#8B8572`, Light Grey `#ECEBE6`
- **Primary typefaces:** Playfair Display (headers, quotes, call-outs — uppercase/title/sentence case, italics and bold both okay), Montserrat (same uses), Montserrat Light (body copy)
- **Secondary typefaces (print only — not freely licensed for web):** Niagara Solid (Messenger, GBH Housing materials), Market Deco (Messenger, always uppercase), "A Love of Thunder" (Fridays at the Farm promo materials)
- **Brand elements:** thin mustard-colored floral line-vector illustrations, used sparingly as dividers/accents, often on white or a mustard tint background
- **Photography direction:** light and airy; people, produce, homes

### For the website specifically
- Lean on the neutral/secondary palette (white, light grey, sage green) as the dominant base, with mustard and the floral linework as accents — not dominant background fills. This was a deliberate shift from the original print materials, which the client felt leaned too heavily on mustard to read as "light, airy, and sophisticated" on screen.
- Texture: subtle procedural grain on solid-color sections, not a tiled photo (a tiled paper-stock photo with a multiply blend looked muddy and showed visible seams — avoid this approach).
- Third accent font for the site: Bebas Neue (Google Fonts, free) for stat numbers and badges — not part of the original print brand guide, chosen because Niagara Solid isn't freely licensed for web use.
