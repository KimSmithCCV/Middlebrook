Live-Work Townhomes

A home upstairs and a storefront on the main level, in a brick building on Middlebrook’s Main Street that you own just like a townhome.

A home and a storefront under one roof

Set along the Town Center’s emerging Main Street, each Live-Work Townhome pairs modern living space upstairs with flexible commercial space on the main level. They are made for entrepreneurs, creatives, and investors looking for a one-of-a-kind ownership opportunity.

The townhomes are designed to support local businesses and the walkable, community-focused life Middlebrook is known for. Live above and work below, skip the commute, and stay connected to the neighborhood. Or lease the storefront or the home on its own.

Four levels, one address

The roof
A private 375 sq ft patio overlooking the Town Center.
The home
About 1,450 sq ft on the second level, with two bedrooms, two baths, open-concept living, a kitchen, and laundry.
The storefront
1,240 sq ft on the main level, delivered as a vanilla shell ready to finish for retail, office, studio, or boutique use. It has its own entrance, a restroom, and separate electrical and mechanical systems.
The lower level
A two-car garage off the back, a mud room, a restroom, and 600 sq ft of bonus space for storage, a gym, or whatever you need.
