## Positioning
- Core differentiator: **multigenerational living** — different price points and product types to fit extended family, carried across website, ads, and social.
- **No individuals named**: do not name the founder (or other individuals) or include personal or family history anywhere on the site. Ideas from the founder letter can be used without attribution.
- **Fair housing guardrail**: never frame a specific home type as being "for" a specific age group or family role (e.g. never say "Cottages are for grandparents"). Describe configuration and function instead — private entrance, single-level living, square footage — and let buyers self-select. This applies to all copy, ads, and social, not just the website.
- Buyers can bring their own builder, but all homes must follow Middlebrook's design guidelines.
- Buyers choose from existing floor plans (not fully custom-from-scratch).
- Status: approximately 7 years into development, roughly 80 homes built so far. This is an established community with real history — lean on that (resident stories, named recurring events with multi-year track records) rather than "founding resident" urgency language. Avoid future-tense copy ("the agrihood will be...") — write in present tense reflecting what already exists.
- Walkability is a core, explicit selling point — Great Western Bike Trail (runs through the center of Middlebrook; say "close access," not "direct access"), Middlebrook Meadow trails, pedestrian-focused Town Center.
- **Garages kept from dominating the streetscape is a big deal to the client.** Give it its own emphasis wherever homes or design guidelines come up (own principle on Vision, called out on the home page, a lead point on Design Guidelines and home type pages).
- Geothermal energy is a significant community feature; surface it throughout home and community content. Use only verified system, usage, and performance figures. Do not claim lower energy use or savings (client removed "substantially reducing energy consumption," October 2026); describe comfort and how the system works instead.
- Tone: sophisticated and established, while keeping a farm-to-table feel. Avoid rustic/cutesy language; let imagery and specific facts (geothermal stats, acreage, named events) carry warmth instead of generic lifestyle language. No em dashes in copy.

## Design principle: no template patterns
Avoid generic UI that reads as AI/template: left-stripe callout boxes, equal three-card grids, centered icon stacks. Use brand-specific treatments instead, e.g. the stamped status tag (`.season-stamp`, Niagara in an outlined mustard tag, slightly rotated) with a Playfair italic line (`.season-notice`).

## Hero system (four styles, each with a job)
1. **Home hero** (`.hero`): full-screen photo, headline bottom-left, "This week" panel overlapping its bottom edge. Home page only.
2. **Photo hero** (`.page-hero`): ~640px photo with a dark left-side overlay, headline over the photo, optional play button. For experience and place pages: Vision, Visit, The Farm, Events, Fridays at the Farm, Stay & Rentals.
3. **Split hero** (`.split-hero` + `<body class="nav-light">`): sand text panel left with a spec line and Request Info button, full-color photo bleeding off the right edge. For home type pages: Villas, Cottages, Townhomes, Lofts, Single-Family, Lots.
4. **Text-first hero** (`.text-hero` + `<body class="nav-light">`): large headline and lede on white, then a wide photo overlapping the next section (give that section a sand or sage background and ~160px top padding). For content pages: Blog, Our Team, Press, Design Guidelines, Invest.
Avoid using the same hero style on two pages that sit next to each other in the menu, where possible. For home type pages, use photos of homes without people (fair housing).

## Brand / visual direction
- Primary palette: white/cream base, sage green `#898F70` (chosen by client) carrying structure, mustard `#AC8F5E` pulled back to true accent duty (buttons, dividers, floral linework) rather than a dominant background color, forest green `#3E4332` as the one high-contrast band.
- Subtle texture/grain on solid-color sections rather than flat fills (procedural, not a tiled photo — a tiled photo with a multiply blend created muddy color and visible seams and was reverted).
- Typography: Playfair Display (headers/editorial, from the existing brand guide), Montserrat Regular (body; the brand guide specifies Light, but Light read too thin on screen, so the site uses Regular in a dark gray, `--text-soft: #403f37`), Niagara (accent — badges and photo caption labels, used sparingly; 14px and up only; small labels and dates stay in Montserrat) and Niagara Engraved (nav wordmark, matching the logo), both served from Adobe Fonts web project kit `tcg6hwg` and set as `--font-accent` / `--font-wordmark` in `style.css`. These work only while the Creative Cloud plan that owns the kit stays active; otherwise they fall back to a condensed system font. Bebas Neue (the earlier free stand-in) has been removed. Market Deco is still print-only.
- Photography direction (per brand guide): light and airy; people, produce, homes.
- Floral line-vector motif (thin mustard linework) used sparingly as a divider/accent, pulled from the existing brand guide. All 30 brand sprigs (extracted from the Brand Guide IDML, page 6) are in `images/brand/sprigs/` as horizontal SVGs (sprig-01 to sprig-30); use with `<img class="sprig">`.
- Layout system tokens in `style.css` `:root`: `--gutter`, `--container`, `--overlap`, `--bleed-left`, `--shadow-lift`; shared `.label` class for small uppercase kickers. Grid-breaking moves in use: panel overlapping the hero, Live & Build photo bleeding to the left viewport edge, asymmetric featured testimonial, full-bleed captioned photo (`.photo-bleed`).
- Layout reference liked: overlapping photo + floating text-panel compositions (seen on Serenbe's site) for visual interest, especially useful for photos that aren't strong enough to stand alone as a full hero image — a soft-focus/blurred version can work as atmospheric backdrop behind a sharp focal photo, but this only reads well with real photography, not placeholder graphics.
- Google Fonts confirmed available for both Playfair Display and Montserrat (including the Light weight), no licensing concerns for web use.
