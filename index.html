    Live & Build

    Homes designed for everyday life, close to the people who matter most.

    Middlebrook brings together flexible home types, private entrances, and generous outdoor living, creating a neighborhood that works for growing families, close relatives, and the rhythm of a multigenerational life.

    Choose from move-in ready homes, floor plans, or builders within our design guidelines. Every home follows the same guidelines: front porches face the street, and garages never dominate it.

    Rooted in the land, and in each other

    Middlebrook brings together the comfort of everyday life with the beauty of the outdoors. The community also makes significant use of geothermal energy. Families can gather at The Farm, take a short walk to the Town Garden, and create traditions that stretch across generations.
