Steps from the trail, the Meadow, and the Farm

The Cottages sit along Highway G14 with backyard access to the Great Western Trail, and close access to Middlebrook Meadow and the neighborhood walking paths. Fridays at the Farm, Iowa Distilling, Wilson’s Orchard & Farm, Middlebrook Mercantile, and Town Center are all within walking distance.

A private fenced patio yard

Every cottage has its own fenced-in side patio, ready for dinner outside or a quiet morning coffee. The yard is fully sodded with an irrigation system, and the HOA takes care of lawn care and snow removal.

What comes in every cottage

Inside

  • Luxury vinyl plank flooring in the kitchen, living, dining, and bathrooms
  • Carpet in the bedrooms
  • Slab front cabinetry with quartz countertops in the kitchen and baths
  • Tile backsplash in the kitchen
  • Tile shower with a glass door in the primary bath
  • Elongated, comfort height toilets

Outside

  • James Hardie siding with Chamclad wood-look accents
  • Quaker vinyl windows
  • Private fenced patio with a 10' x 16' concrete patio
  • Fully sodded yard with irrigation, maintained by the HOA
  • Two-car attached garage, 22' x 22'

Energy and comfort

  • Geothermal heating and cooling
  • 2x6 construction with batt insulation
  • R-40 attic insulation
  • Programmable thermostat
  • 40-gallon electric water heater
  • GE microwave, electric range, and dishwasher included
