Blog · Events · October 2026

Porchfest 2026 in photos

Six porches, six bands, and a whole neighborhood in lawn chairs. Here is how the first Middlebrook Porchfest went.

On Sunday, October 11, neighbors in Great Western Crossing opened up their porches to local musicians for the very first Middlebrook Porchfest. From 11 in the morning until 6 at night, the music moved house to house, and so did everyone else, lawn chairs in hand.

Porch to porch

Six sets ran one after another, so nobody had to miss a band. The day started on Callison Avenue with Randy Burke & the Prisoners, then Heath Alan and Bonne Finken. Other Brothers played North 38th Street, and The Soul Searchers took a back porch on North Brier Lane.

Plenty of people came by bike, and food trucks parked at Mayor Tom Becker Park, right in the middle of the route.

The Farmhouse finale

When the neighborhood sets wrapped up, everyone headed to the Farmhouse porch for a two-hour closing show with Weary Ramblers, under the oak and the string lights, with food trucks and the Wine Wagen pouring until 6.

Thank you

A big thank you to every host family who opened up their porch, to the bands, and to everyone who came out. Porchfest is coming back in fall 2027. Want to host a porch or play a set? Let us know at Events@MiddlebrookFarm.com.
