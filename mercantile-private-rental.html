Cumming’s historic one-room schoolhouse, restored as a bar and gathering place, with a full bar, a cozy brick interior, and a patio outside.

Reserving your date

Send a request through the Mercantile’s booking form and the team will follow up. Once you review and sign the contract, a $300 deposit holds your date. The deposit is non-refundable within 30 days of your event, and the balance is paid on the day.
