The town garden

Middlebrook’s two-acre town garden is a place where agriculture, community, and nature come together at the heart of the Agrihood.

Middlebrook’s agrarian lifestyle centers on enriching residents’ quality of life by providing access to fresh local foods, promoting wellness, and fostering community connections. In each residential neighborhood, you’ll find access to agricultural enclaves, natural areas, interconnected trails, and the convenience of a pedestrian-focused town center.

Whether you’re harvesting flowers, tending a garden plot, shopping for fresh produce, or exploring the meadow, the town garden offers opportunities to slow down, reconnect with nature, and experience that lifestyle firsthand at the center of our modern, walkable community.

    A living laboratory

    Growing better soil, season after season

    The town garden also serves as a living laboratory for innovative and regenerative growing practices. Through on-farm experimentation and education, we explore techniques that improve soil health, reduce inputs, and build resilient growing systems.

    Current efforts include planting directly into crimped cover crops, a conservation practice that helps suppress weeds, retain soil moisture, reduce erosion, and enhance soil biology while minimizing soil disturbance. These practices reflect our commitment to stewarding the land responsibly while demonstrating sustainable approaches to food production for our community.

    Our Farm Manager, Mosa, went looking for better ways to farm Iowa soil, and every gardener and Farm Stand shopper benefits from what he finds. When you find someone who cares this much about the work, you give him room to dig in.

    Shop Our Fresh Produce

    We offer a diverse range of produce and plantings vary by season, all grown right here in Middlebrook following organic gardening practices. Fresh produce and eggs are available seasonally at the Middlebrook Farm Stand, and plant starts are offered during our annual spring plant sale in May.

    Unwind in the Pollinator Habitat

    The pollinator habitat, an area designated for native perennial plant growth in Middlebrook’s town garden, represents an important step in our commitment to stewardship and sustainability. As it matures, it will provide habitat for birds, bees, butterflies, and other beneficial wildlife while creating a beautiful, ever-changing backdrop eventually featuring walking paths, gathering spaces, and seasonal beauty for the community to enjoy.

    Grow With Us

    Middlebrook offers 4’x8’ community garden plots right in our beautiful town garden, perfect for gardeners of all experience levels. Choose an outdoor plot just west of the you-pick flower field, or an indoor plot inside our Community High Tunnel.

    Grow year-round Indoor plots in the Community High Tunnel stay protected from Iowa frost, so you can keep harvesting through fall and winter.

    Plots are $100 for the calendar year (January–December 2026) and include free water access plus expert tips and guidance from our Farm Manager, Mosa.

    Full for 2026 Garden plots are spoken for this season. Check back next spring for updates!

    Cut & Create Your Own Bouquet

    Visitors can create their own bouquets in-hand in Middlebrook’s you-pick flower field. Instructions and supplies can be found in the Farm Stand, and our native perennial and annual flowers are at their peak bloom June through September.

    For those who want to enjoy fresh flowers all season long, our seasonal You-Pick Flower Membership includes access to multiple bouquet harvests throughout the summer. Memberships are $100 per season.

    Full for 2026 Flower memberships are spoken for this season. Check back next spring for updates!

    The Middlebrook Chickens

    The hardest workers on The Farm

    Our free-range flock lives in the little red coop on The Farm, and nothing from the garden goes to waste on their watch. The split tomatoes, outer cabbage leaves, and other leftovers from the harvest go straight to the chickens.

    They return the favor with fresh eggs for the Farm Stand. When you see a dozen on the shelf, grab it.
