Main Street is open for business

The Town Center is already home to the Middlebrook Lofts and their shops, the Peoples Company headquarters, where 75 people work every day, and the Live-Work Townhomes. Neighbors walk and bike in from the homes around it, and the Great Western Trail runs right past.

Next up is Lofts 2.0: a three-story building on Main Street with 32 apartments upstairs and storefronts on the ground floor, ready for retail, dining, services, and offices.
