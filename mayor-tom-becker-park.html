Visit · Outdoors

Mayor Tom Becker Park

Climb the windmill, explore the play barn, and take the wheel of a tractor, all in the heart of Great Western Crossing.

The park

A one-of-a-kind park featuring an agriculture-inspired playground, large open spaces, and charming landscaping.

The park offers an escape for all ages and abilities to explore, discover, and relax at their own leisure. When it is time for a break, stretch out on the lawn, gather under the picnic pavilion, or take a lap around the walking loop.

Location N. Brier Lane, Great Western Crossing See where it is in Middlebrook

What you will find

Plenty to do, and places to rest

Play

  • Playground scavenger hunt
  • Slides and climbing structures
  • A separate play structure for toddlers
  • Play tractors
  • Swings for big kids and babies, plus inclusive swings
  • Soft wooden mulch

Relax

  • Walking loop
  • Benches
  • Covered picnic pavilion
  • Large open lawns

Good to know

  • Parking on site
  • Bathrooms, open seasonally
  • Drinking fountain, open seasonally

A short walk from home

Mayor Tom Becker Park sits within Great Western Crossing, connected to the rest of Middlebrook by sidewalks and trails. Pack a picnic for the pavilion, or stop by on the way to The Farm.
