A busy corner, ready to build

Lot 2 sits on the south side of Highway G14, between Interstate 35 and the Middlebrook Agrihood. Traffic heading to the interstate, the Hy-Vee Distribution Center, and Middlebrook passes right by. The ground is ready for whatever you have in mind, with no waiting on us to build first.

Access
A full access road off Highway G14, with a cross-access easement to 15th Ave.
Utilities
City water and sewer are available.
Neighbors
Casey’s next door, Starbucks and Hy-Vee Fast & Fresh nearby, and the Hy-Vee Distribution Center across the road.
Zoning
