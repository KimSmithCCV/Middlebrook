Dream it, build it: a bike shop on Main Street

The Great Western Trail runs right through the Town Center. All it needs is a bike shop, and the perfect spot is for sale.

Every day, riders roll through Middlebrook on the Great Western Trail, the paved path on the old Chicago Great Western rail line that runs from Cumming to Des Moines. It passes right through the Town Center, a few steps from Main Street. Picture a shop where they can stop for a tune-up, a new tube, or a cold drink, and where neighbors buy their kids’ first bikes.

That is one of the ideas on the Middlebrook Vision Board, our collection of businesses we would love to see here. And one of the best places to start is already built.

Live above, work below

The Live-Work Townhomes on Main Street put your home and your business in one building, and one unit is available now. Here is how a bike shop could fit:

  • The storefront. About 1,240 square feet on the main level, with its own entrance, delivered as a vanilla shell ready to finish as your showroom.
  • The lower level. A two-car garage and 600 square feet of bonus space: room for a repair bench, inventory, and a rental fleet.
  • The home. About 1,450 square feet upstairs, with two bedrooms and two baths, and a private rooftop patio overlooking the Town Center.

You own the building fee simple, just like a townhome, and you can lease either the home or the storefront on its own if your plans change. The unit is listed at $850,000.

Good neighbors

A bike shop would not be alone. Cups & Cones, Reese Marie Salon, and The Farmstand are already open next door, FreeBird & Co and Bloom Play Co. are on the way, and the Town Garden, Mayor Tom Becker Park, and Fridays at the Farm are all a short ride away.

Not a bike person?

The same space could be a flower shop stocked from the Town Garden, a yoga or fitness studio, a bakery, or an office for your design or real estate business. The Vision Board has more ideas, from a neighborhood gym to a diner and drive-in. If you dream it, you can build it.
