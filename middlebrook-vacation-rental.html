Steps from the Mercantile

The Vacation Rental is steps away from happy hour drinks at the Middlebrook Mercantile, Cumming’s historic one-room schoolhouse. Restored and reopened as a wine and cocktail bar, it greets you at night with exclusive wines and flavor-forward cocktails.

Also close by: the Great Western Trail, Mayor Tom Becker Park, Middlebrook Meadow, and the Town Garden.
