The Villas at Great Western Crossing

Single-family home ownership with a smaller footprint and less maintenance. Zero-entry villas with the primary suite on the main level, a lower level you can finish now or later, and lawn care and snow removal covered by the HOA.

  • Zero entry
  • Main-level primary suite
  • 1,381-1,472 SF
Request Info See villas for sale Find it on the map

Craftsman bungalows on a walkable street

The Villas, built by Ground Breaker Homes, take their cue from the craftsman bungalows of Des Moines’ historic neighborhoods: covered front porches and gabled rooflines. The Villas are zero entry, with only a minimal step between the front walk and the main floor.

Choose a villa plan, or design your own

Every plan puts two bedrooms, two baths, and the primary suite on the main level, with a two-car garage. Finish the lower level to add two more bedrooms, an extra bathroom, and a family room.

Exteriors are customized, so no two villas on the street look alike.

Or design your own villa

Start from one of the plans above, or create your own. Every villa is built to the Villa design standards, so the street keeps its character no matter who builds.

  1. 01

    Work with our architect and Ground Breaker Homes to design a villa around how you live.

  2. 02

    Bring your preferred builder. Any builder can build in the Villas, following the same Villa design standards.

Everything you need on one level

  • Open great room with 10 ft ceilings and a gas fireplace
  • Private primary suite with its own bath, on the main level
  • Laundry right next to the bedrooms
  • Rear deck just off the kitchen and dining area
  • A window bay in the great room, or a pocket office niche

A lower level, finished now or later

Every villa comes with an unfinished lower level. Finish it from the start with two more bedrooms, a full bath, and a family room, or leave it for later and finish it when you need the space.

Lawn care and snow removal are covered by the HOA.
