The Foxglove is a single-story home with clean-lined modern design and a focus on comfort, simplicity, and thoughtful everyday living. Its 1,799 square feet strike a balance of openness, privacy, and effortless single-level living.

The heart of the home is a dramatic vaulted living room, with a fireplace feature wall that makes a warm focal point for gathering. The dining room and kitchen include a comfortable sitting nook for morning coffee, conversation, or everyday moments.

Clean architecture, careful proportions, and a modern approach to livability make it a refined yet welcoming home for those who want timeless simplicity with contemporary character.

The Norman is a timeless Tudor-inspired home that blends classic architectural character with modern-day livability. Planned as a one-story home, it offers well-balanced living space for those who appreciate style, comfort, and efficiency.

Inside are two spacious main-level bedrooms and a versatile office for working from home or pursuing hobbies. The vaulted living room ceiling creates an open, airy feel, while exposed beams in the kitchen and dining room add warmth.

From the striking stone exterior and half-timbered gables to the bright, inviting interior, The Norman pairs enduring charm with modern comfort.

Blending traditional charm with modern convenience, The Morris is made for today’s lifestyle.

The main level centers on a spacious living room and kitchen, connected by a pass-through scullery and walk-in pantry that lead to a separate, formal dining room, perfect for entertaining or quiet family meals.

Upstairs, a well-appointed primary suite and two more bedrooms make a private, peaceful retreat from the main living areas. With its classic exterior and efficient layout, The Morris delivers timeless appeal and practical elegance.

The Piedmont is a striking two-story farmhouse inspired by Greek Revival design, blending classic architectural elegance with modern livability, for those who want timeless style with everyday comfort.

The main level is anchored by a gracious living room with 12-foot coffered ceilings. The kitchen flows into a butler’s pantry and on to a dedicated dining room, a layout made for entertaining, celebrations, or family meals. A spacious primary suite on the main level is a private retreat with the ease of single-level living.

With its balanced layout, elegant details, and enduring farmhouse character, The Piedmont is a sophisticated yet welcoming home designed to stand the test of time.
